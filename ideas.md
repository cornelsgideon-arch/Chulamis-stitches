## Chosen approach: Modern Atelier

### Design Movement

The revised website draws from a **modern atelier lookbook**: an uncluttered black-and-ivory fashion storefront with large photography, direct actions, and a mobile-first rhythm. It retains the cultural specificity of Nigerian bespoke tailoring while removing the overly dense editorial treatment that compromised mobile usability.

### Core Principles

1. **Mobile clarity first.** One strong action, readable type, and predictable spacing take priority on small screens.
2. **Garment-led presence.** Large cropped fashion images lead each decision; supporting copy stays quiet.
3. **Quiet contrast.** Ink-black, warm paper, and a single gold accent create hierarchy without visual clutter.
4. **Direct utility.** Collection browsing and WhatsApp booking require minimal steps.

### Color Philosophy

The near-black **Atelier Ink** background conveys privacy, precision, and confidence. Soft ivory is used for readability and warmth rather than pure white. A restrained antique gold serves as a maker's mark: it appears only where the eye needs to understand action, selection, or craftsmanship. Deep plum surfaces echo the studio energy in the supplied fashion imagery without competing with the garments.

### Layout Paradigm

The page uses a **stacked mobile lookbook**: compact hero, short brand introduction, a swipeable collection rail that becomes a grid on desktop, a three-step process, and a direct booking section. This makes the experience feel intentional in one hand and expansive on a large screen.

### Signature Elements

1. A slim antique-gold line that marks section transitions.
2. Small uppercase collection metadata paired with large, calm garment names.
3. A black image frame with a single off-white corner registration mark.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interaction should feel **considered and direct**. On mobile, collection cards swipe naturally as a rail. On desktop, they resolve into a simple, scannable grid. The booking form opens a pre-composed WhatsApp enquiry rather than pretending to automate a studio fitting.

### Animation

Motion is limited to 180–280 ms, using a sharp ease-out. Gallery cards lift a few pixels and subtly change their image crop on hover. Section copy fades up once on entry; no perpetual animation is used. The navigation surface becomes opaque after the visitor begins scrolling. All nonessential motion yields to reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

**Cormorant Garamond** is used for large, expressive serif headlines and select editorial statements; its varied strokes give the site fashion authority. **Manrope** is the precise modern sans for navigation, labels, body copy, prices, and forms. Headlines use generous negative tracking; all metadata is uppercase and spaced widely for clarity.

### Brand Essence

**Chulamis Stitches creates culturally rooted, made-to-measure clothing for people who want their presence tailored with intent.** Personality: **discerning, composed, expressive**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are concise, assured, and sensory. Calls to action are framed as personal studio invitations, not generic conversion language. Microcopy is specific about the bespoke process.

> “Tailored for the room you have not entered yet.”

> “Send your occasion. We will shape the rest.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark pairs a quietly spaced sans “CHULAMIS” with a smaller serif “STITCHES.” The symbol is a continuous gold thread that becomes a C-shaped stitch and a needle: compact enough for the favicon while expressive enough for the header.

### Signature Brand Color

**Atelier Gold — #C79B4B.** It is the single, ownable signal of hand-finished value across the interface.
